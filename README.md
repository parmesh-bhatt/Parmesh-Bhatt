## 🚀 Top Projects

### 🪔 [RitualPlanner](https://github.com/RitualPlanner)

**Next.js · TypeScript · NestJS · PostgreSQL · TypeORM · Redis · BullMQ · Docker**

A modern SaaS platform designed to simplify the day-to-day work of
Karmakand professionals by bringing traditional ritual management into
a structured digital workflow.

**Highlights**

- 📅 Ritual and task scheduling
- 👥 Client and co-priest management
- 🧾 Billing and payment management
- 💰 Expense tracking
- 📝 Notes and ritual templates
- 🔔 Reminders and notifications
- 📊 Dashboard and analytics
- 🔒 Secure authentication

> Building technology that supports tradition without replacing it.

---

### 🗓️ [Dainik Panchang](https://github.com/RitualPlanner/dainik-panchang)

**Next.js · React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · shadcn/ui**

A Panchang application created to generate and share daily Panchang
information in a structured and accessible format.

**Highlights**

- 📅 Daily Panchang generation
- 🌐 Multilingual support
- 📄 PDF generation
- 🔗 QR code sharing
- 🔎 OCR-powered image extraction

---

### 🪑 [Furniture Rentals](https://github.com/parmesh-bhatt/Furniture-Rentals)

**React · Express.js · MongoDB · Tailwind CSS**

A full-stack furniture rental platform developed during a
36-hour national hackathon.

**Highlights**

- 🔐 JWT-based authentication
- 🛋️ Furniture rental management
- 💳 Razorpay payment integration
- 📦 Rental tracking
- 👥 Collaborative development with a 3-member team
